This repository hosts all of the Python code I wrote as part of research I conducted in reinforcement learning. I leveraged the Gymnasium API to simulate a humanoid environment (namely, the Half-Cheetah environment) and iterated across various learning rates to optimize based on the largest episodic return. I iterated through the learning rates: [1e −2 , 5e −3 , 1e −3 , 5e −4 , 1e −4 , 5e −5 , 1e −5 , 5e −6 , 1e −6 , 5e −7], and ultimately discovered that in tandem with the Policy Proximal Optimization, the best performing learning rate was the 0.0001, with a normalized episodic return of 1 at 45,000 steps. A close contender was the learning rate of 0.0005. The worst performing learning rate was 5e-7, with an episodic return of -0.31.
